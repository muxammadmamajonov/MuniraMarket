# Demo product photos

Only Unsplash (Unsplash License) and Pexels (Pexels License) photos: both allow free commercial
use without attribution; credit is kept here anyway. No Unsplash+ (premium) photos, no shop images.

Each photo is a square crop, re-encoded as WebP (quality 80, metadata stripped) at 800px
(`<slug>.webp`) and 400px (`<slug>-400.webp`), served from `/products/`.
